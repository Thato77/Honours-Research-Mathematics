# Honours-Research-Mathematics
Research in Functional Analysis and Topology exploring advanced mathematical concepts, theoretical results and rigorous mathematical proofs/arguments as part of a BSc Honours degree in Mathematics and Applied Mathematics.

## Statement of the Problem.

Given two compact metric spaces $X$ and $Y$, with the respective spaces of continuous functions $C(X)$ and $C(Y)$. 

$X$ is **Homeomorphic** to $Y$ if and only if $C(X)$ and $C(Y)$ are **Isomorphic**.

## What we Aim to Prove

We prove the the forward and converse of the the Problem Statement.
